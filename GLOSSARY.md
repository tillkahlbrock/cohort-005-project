# Course Platform

The course platform sells courses to learners. This glossary defines the terms for purchases and the return of money.

## Language

### Refunds

**Refund Request**:
A learner's request to undo a whole purchase and get the money back. The team reviews each Refund Request and approves or declines it.
_Avoid_: Cancellation, Reversal, Chargeback

**Refund**:
The money that goes back to the learner when the team approves a Refund Request.
_Avoid_: Reversal, Chargeback

**Refund Window**:
The period after a purchase in which a learner can make a Refund Request.
_Avoid_: Cancellation window
